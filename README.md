  # DeltaTune Lab

  **Efficient intelligence. Careful experiments. Open research.**

  DeltaTune Lab is an independent AI research lab founded by Pakistan-based researchers **Rao Muhammad Dayan Atif** and **Syed Muhammad Sarim Ahsan**. The lab explores practical ways to adapt, improve, and run modern AI systems with less unnecessary overhead.

  ## Research focus

  - Efficient fine-tuning and model adaptation
  - Reinforcement learning and post-training
  - Compact model reasoning
  - Model optimization and distillation
  - GPU systems, kernel fusion, and memory efficiency

  Our research is guided by three questions:

  1. How much really needs to change?
  2. What makes a small model reason better?
  3. Where is the compute going?
